The current implementation is technically correct, but the generated

PROBLEM

The current prompt asks for:

"3–6 short sentences"
and
"a plain-language explanation of what this brief means"

This causes OpenAI to generate another general summary such as:

"Systemet venter på vigtige økonomiske begivenheder og selskabsresultater..."

That is too generic.

The user can already read the Command Brief headline.

The purpose of "What This Means" is different:

It should HELP THE USER UNDERSTAND THE INDIVIDUAL IMPORTANT LINES
shown in the Command Brief.


REQUIRED BEHAVIOR

whatThisMeans should act as a short explanatory walkthrough of the
important Command Brief items.

For each important item shown in the brief, explain:

1. What the item actually means in ordinary language.
2. Why the system considers it important.
3. What the current system state means for the user.
4. If relevant, what the system is waiting for before reassessing it.

Do NOT merely restate or translate the original sentence.


EXAMPLE

Suppose the Command Brief contains:

State Employment and Unemployment Report in 5 days could impact market conditions.

KEYS:
Keysight Technologies earnings in 2 days; system is waiting for event before reassessment.

SERV:
Serve Robotics faces high financial and execution risks.

0700:
Wait for Earnings in 1d before reassessment — Catalyst Intelligence sees a positive setup.


A Danish whatThisMeans should be closer to:

"Økonomiske begivenheder: Om 5 dage kommer nye amerikanske arbejdsmarkedstal.
De kan påvirke markedet og er en af grundene til, at systemet er forsigtigt
lige nu.

Keysight: Systemet ser positive tegn før regnskabet om 2 dage. Trade Decision
siger dog WaitForEvent, hvilket betyder, at systemet ikke vil tage stilling
endnu. Det vil først se regnskabet og derefter vurdere aktien igen.

Serve Robotics: Systemet vurderer fortsat selskabet som risikabelt på grund
af svag økonomi og usikkerhed omkring selskabets udvikling. Det er derfor en
position, der kræver ekstra opmærksomhed.

Tencent: Tencent har regnskab i morgen. Catalyst Intelligence ser positive
tegn, men Trade Decision siger stadig WaitForEvent. Det betyder, at Tencent
er interessant at følge tæt, men systemet anbefaler ikke handling før
regnskabet."


IMPORTANT SEMANTIC RULE

The explanation must preserve the hierarchy:

Catalyst Intelligence:
WHY a company/event may be interesting.

Opportunity Finder:
HOW attractive an opportunity currently appears.

Trade Decision:
WHAT the system currently intends to do about it.

Trade Review:
WHETHER there is an actionable trade NOW.

Explain these relationships naturally when relevant.


WAIT FOR EVENT

This is especially important.

When an item has:

decision = WaitForEvent

the explanation must make clear in ordinary language:

- the company may still look interesting or positive,
- but the system is intentionally waiting,
- what event it is waiting for,
- and that the company will be reassessed after the event.

Never turn positive Catalyst sentiment into an implied recommendation.


PREPARE TO BUY

If Trade Decision says PrepareToBuy, explain that this is stronger than merely
monitoring an opportunity, but it still does NOT mean a trade is ready now
unless Trade Review says it is actionable.


TRADE REVIEW

If readyTradeCount = 0, the explanation should make it understandable that
nothing currently requires approval.

For Danish, natural wording could be:

"Der er derfor ikke nogen handel, du skal godkende eller tage stilling til lige nu."

Do not mechanically repeat this if the Action Status directly below already
makes it obvious and it would make the explanation unnecessarily repetitive.


FORMAT

Do NOT force whatThisMeans into one generic paragraph.

It may use short paragraphs separated by newline characters.

Prefer one short explanatory paragraph per important Command Brief item.

Use the company name or understandable subject at the beginning when helpful:

"Keysight: ..."
"Serve Robotics: ..."
"NVIDIA: ..."
"Økonomiske begivenheder: ..."

This makes it easy for the user to connect the explanation to the lines above.

Do not use JSON arrays or introduce additional schema fields.

whatThisMeans remains ONE string.

Use newline characters inside the string for paragraph separation.


LENGTH

Do not use the existing rigid "3–6 sentences" requirement.

Instead:

Explain the important items shown in the Command Brief.

Normally approximately 1–2 short sentences per important item.

Keep the entire explanation concise enough to scan quickly, but prioritize
understanding over an arbitrary sentence count.
