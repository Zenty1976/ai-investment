Portfolio Manager v2 has a good technical foundation, but it does not yet fully implement the intended CIO role.

Keep the current modular structure, snapshot version guards, repository keys and existing UI.

Make the following correctness and architecture improvements.

--------------------------------------------------
1. Consume all required strategic modules
--------------------------------------------------

The CIO target synthesis currently consumes only:

- Portfolio Analyzer
- Risk Analyzer
- Opportunity Finder

It must also consume relevant structured data from:

- Company Monitor v2
- Trade Decision Engine
- Sector Monitor
- Market Alerts
- Market Monitor

Use Company Monitor data for:

- investmentView
- investmentCaseStrength
- investmentCaseChange
- thesis statuses
- confidence
- key risks and catalysts

Use Trade Decision data for:

- decision type
- readiness
- blockedByEvent
- blocking reason
- evidence band
- confidence
- target allocation guidance when available

Use Sector Monitor for actual sector attractiveness and direction.

Use Market Alerts only when alerts materially affect a current holding or target candidate.

Use Market Monitor for broad risk posture and cash-target context.

Only include Company Monitor analyses for:

- current holdings
- active Opportunity Finder candidates
- active Trade Decision subjects

Use the shared Company Identity service.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Extend TargetAllocation
--------------------------------------------------

Add these structured fields to each target allocation:

- conviction: High | Medium | Low
- supportingModules: module enum array
- blockingFactors: string[]
- allocationStatus:
  StrategicTarget | Provisional | Blocked | Excluded
- reasonForStatus

Definitions:

StrategicTarget:
A valid desired long-term allocation.

Provisional:
The company may belong in the portfolio, but important evidence is incomplete.

Blocked:
A future event or critical condition prevents current deployment.

Excluded:
The company was considered but should not currently receive capital.

Only StrategicTarget allocations should be treated as immediately deployable.

Provisional and Blocked allocations may remain visible in the target portfolio but must not become current capital-allocation suggestions.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Integrate Trade Decision readiness into capital allocation
--------------------------------------------------

computeCapitalAllocation must not allocate deployable cash merely because a ticker is below its target.

A capital-allocation item may be actionable only when:

- allocationStatus === StrategicTarget
- the corresponding Trade Decision is ReadyForReview when a trade decision exists
- blockedByEvent === false
- required Company Monitor data is present and fresh
- no critical blocking factor exists

For Provisional or Blocked allocations:

- suggestedAmountBase must be zero
- do not include them in totalSuggestedDeploymentBase
- expose them separately as future or blocked allocation gaps

Add to CapitalAllocationPlan:

- actionableItems
- blockedItems
- provisionalItems

Do not suggest deploying cash into an event-blocked candidate.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Fix sector classification
--------------------------------------------------

Do not use assetType or exchange as a sector proxy.

Stock, ETF, NASDAQ, NYSE and OMX are not sectors.

Resolve sectors using this priority:

1. Company Monitor company.sector for matched companies
2. Opportunity Finder sector for candidates
3. A shared instrument/company metadata repository
4. Unknown

Calculate sector weights only from actual sector labels.

Do not award a strong Sector Balance score when sector information is mostly Unknown.

Expose:

- classifiedPositionPercent
- unknownSectorPercent

If sector coverage is insufficient, mark the Sector Balance score as low-confidence or unavailable rather than calculating a misleading precise score.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Redesign deterministic health sub-scores
--------------------------------------------------

Health should measure portfolio construction quality, not recent investment luck or account housekeeping.

Remove P&L Quality from the overall Portfolio Health score.

Unrealised profit or loss may be shown separately, but it should not make portfolio construction healthier or less healthy.

Remove Account Health from the overall score.

An empty account is not inherently a portfolio-quality problem.

Avoid double-counting position count through both:

- Diversification
- Position Count

Use one position-spread measure or clearly give them different purposes with lower combined weight.

The health score should focus on:

- single-position concentration
- top-position concentration
- sector concentration
- currency concentration
- cash alignment versus strategic target
- event concentration
- risk concentration
- conviction alignment
- stale or missing analytical coverage
- diversification quality

Use actual Risk Analyzer and Company Monitor data where relevant.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Replace the current replacement detector
--------------------------------------------------

Do not compare:

Portfolio Analyzer attention converted to 30/55/75

against:

Opportunity Finder overallScore

These are not equivalent metrics.

High attention does not mean a holding is weak.

Build replacement comparisons from comparable structured evidence.

For the held company use:

- Company Monitor investmentCaseStrength
- investmentView
- investmentCaseChange
- thesis invalidation/weakening
- ticker-specific Risk Analyzer severity
- Trade Decision direction and evidence band

For the candidate use:

- Opportunity Finder overallScore
- Company Monitor investmentCaseStrength when available
- Trade Decision evidence when available
- portfolio fit and diversification benefit

A replacement opportunity should require:

- the candidate has stronger comparable evidence
- the existing holding has a genuine weakness or lower strategic fit
- the replacement improves portfolio construction
- no blocking event prevents the candidate
- evidence quality is sufficient on both sides

If Company Monitor is missing for either side, mark the comparison as Provisional rather than presenting it as a valid replacement.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Add input freshness and provenance
--------------------------------------------------

Before synthesising a target portfolio, check freshness of every material source.

Store in the v2 result:

- sourceModulesUsed
- sourceUpdatedAt
- staleSources
- missingSources
- targetConfidence

Do not present a high-confidence target portfolio when critical inputs are stale or missing.

If critical sources are stale:

- allow a Provisional target
- do not create actionable capital-allocation suggestions
- let Orchestrator refresh dependencies first when possible

--------------------------------------------------
8. Avoid unnecessary AI target synthesis
--------------------------------------------------

Do not run a new AI CIO pass after every snapshot timestamp update.

Create a deterministic input fingerprint based on material CIO inputs:

- positions and quantities
- cash and portfolio value within a meaningful tolerance
- latest relevant Company Monitor versions
- Risk Analyzer material result
- Portfolio Analyzer material result
- Opportunity Finder candidate versions
- Trade Decision versions/readiness
- Sector Monitor material result
- Market Alerts meaningful version
- selected policy profile

If the fingerprint is unchanged:

- reuse the current Target Portfolio
- recompute only deterministic current-value-dependent components when needed
- do not call OpenAI

Examples of deterministic recomputation:

- current allocation percentages
- drift
- cash percentage
- capital amounts
- health metrics based on current prices

Only regenerate the AI target when strategic inputs changed materially or a scheduled safety refresh is due.

The Automation Orchestrator should remain the central scheduling authority.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Improve target persistence and history
--------------------------------------------------

Current history stores health and drift counts but not enough information to understand strategic changes.

Keep the compact history, but add:

- target version or fingerprint
- target confidence
- cash target
- target allocations as compact ticker/percent/role/status records
- source freshness summary
- major portfolio changes
- strategic rationale summary

Keep the latest bounded history.

Do not store full prompts or raw OpenAI responses.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Strengthen target validation
--------------------------------------------------

Keep the current allowed-ticker, duplicate, role and total-allocation validation.

Also validate:

- conviction enum
- allocationStatus consistency
- Blocked requires at least one blockingFactor
- StrategicTarget must not have a critical blocking factor
- supportingModules contains only modules actually supplied
- target ranges remain internally valid after normalization
- final equity plus cash equals 100 within a small rounding tolerance

Do not silently convert an unknown AI role to CoreHolding.

Unknown roles should cause validation failure and retry, because silently changing a role can materially alter its allowed allocation range.

--------------------------------------------------
11. Preserve responsibilities
--------------------------------------------------

Portfolio Manager v2 decides:

“What should the portfolio ideally look like?”

It must not:

- generate executable orders
- bypass Trade Decision readiness
- approve trades
- allocate money to blocked decisions
- call Saxo order endpoints

Trade Decision remains responsible for determining whether an individual trade is sufficiently supported and ready.

Trade Review remains responsible for manual approval.

Preserve:

- current snapshot fetching
- multi-account and currency handling
- mock mode
- async snapshot version guards
- existing modular engine files
- current UI
- System Log
- Orchestrator integration
