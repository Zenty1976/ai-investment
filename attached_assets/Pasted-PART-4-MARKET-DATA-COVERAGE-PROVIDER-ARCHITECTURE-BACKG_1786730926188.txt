PART 4 — MARKET DATA COVERAGE & PROVIDER ARCHITECTURE

BACKGROUND

Catalyst Intelligence Parts 1–3 are now substantially complete.

We now have an autonomous pipeline capable of:

Market Universe
→ Catalyst/Event/Signal discovery
→ Screening
→ Driver research
→ Signal accumulation
→ Deep Catalyst Analysis
→ Opportunity Finder
→ Trade Decision

The next problem is DATA COVERAGE.

The intelligence engine cannot discover opportunities that are absent from its
market/event/fundamental data.

Do NOT add more Catalyst reasoning features in this task.

The purpose of Part 4 is:

1. audit our actual current data coverage
2. use existing sources better where possible
3. create clean provider abstractions for missing data
4. make the application ready for broad DK + US coverage
5. NEVER allow OpenAI to invent objective financial data.

==================================================
9. WHY ESTIMATE REVISIONS MATTER
==================================================

The architecture should support questions such as:

"Analysts still expect EPS 10,
but estimates have moved from 8 to 10 over the last 30 days."

This is different from:

"Consensus = 10."

The direction and velocity of expectations can be an important pre-earnings
signal.

Create data structures capable of preserving estimate history rather than only
the latest consensus snapshot.
