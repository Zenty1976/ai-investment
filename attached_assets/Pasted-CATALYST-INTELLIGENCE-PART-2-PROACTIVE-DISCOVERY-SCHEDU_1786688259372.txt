==================================================
1. FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE IN DISCOVERY
==================================================

Current Catalyst Intelligence candidate discovery appears to rely mainly on:

- portfolio holdings
- Opportunity Finder candidates
- existing Company Monitor entries

This is insufficient.

It creates a circular problem:

Catalyst Intelligence only investigates companies already considered interesting.

But one of Catalyst Intelligence's purposes is to DISCOVER companies that should
become interesting.

Fix this.

Catalyst discovery must be able to introduce NEW companies into the intelligence
pipeline.

The conceptual flow should become:

SUPPORTED MARKET UNIVERSE

→ upcoming potentially market-moving events

→ cheap deterministic/relevance screening

→ potentially interesting companies

→ Catalyst Intelligence

→ Opportunity Finder

→ Trade Decision

A company must NOT need to already exist in:

Portfolio
Opportunity Finder
or Company Monitor

to become a Catalyst candidate.

==================================================
4. SCHEDULED EVENT DISCOVERY
==================================================

This is extremely important.

The system must actively search for known upcoming potentially market-moving
company events.

Example conceptually:

"Company X will hold an investor event in 5 days."

This alone does NOT mean BUY.

But it means:

"This company may deserve pre-event investigation."

Event Monitor should discover and maintain such events before they happen.

Use cheap structured sources first where available.

Use web discovery only where necessary.

Do not repeatedly rediscover unchanged events.

Use state/fingerprints.


==================================================
5. PRE-EVENT INTELLIGENCE
==================================================

For a potentially important scheduled event, Catalyst Intelligence should investigate:

WHAT IS KNOWN TO HAPPEN?

WHAT IS EXPECTED TO BE DISCUSSED/ANNOUNCED?

WHAT HAS MANAGEMENT OFFICIALLY SAID?

WHAT DO RELIABLE REPORTERS EXPECT?

ARE THERE CREDIBLE RUMORS?

ARE THERE INDUSTRY/SUPPLIER/PARTNER SIGNALS?

HAS MANAGEMENT BEEN TEASING SOMETHING?

HAS THE COMPANY RECENTLY MADE RELATED ANNOUNCEMENTS?

ARE THERE UNUSUAL CHANGES IN RELEVANT COMPANY DRIVERS?

HOW HAS THE STOCK BEHAVED LEADING INTO THE EVENT?

DOES THE MARKET APPEAR TO HAVE ALREADY PRICED IN POSITIVE EXPECTATIONS?


==================================================
6. FACT VS EXPECTATION VS RUMOR
==================================================

Do NOT mix these.

Every pre-event information item must be classified.

Suggested categories:

CONFIRMED_FACT
OFFICIAL_EXPECTATION
RELIABLE_REPORTING
ANALYST_EXPECTATION
INDUSTRY_SIGNAL
CREDIBLE_RUMOR
UNVERIFIED_RUMOR
AI_INTERPRETATION

Example:

CONFIRMED_FACT:
Company event scheduled for Tuesday.

RELIABLE_REPORTING:
Reuters/Bloomberg reports that a new product announcement is expected.

CREDIBLE_RUMOR:
Multiple independent sources suggest feature X.

UNVERIFIED_RUMOR:
Single low-confidence social-media claim.

AI_INTERPRETATION:
Combined evidence suggests the event may be more significant than normal.

AI_INTERPRETATION must NEVER be stored or displayed as a confirmed fact.


==================================================
7. SOURCE INDEPENDENCE
==================================================

Do not count article quantity as evidence quantity.

If:

Reuters publishes one report

and 12 websites repeat the Reuters report

that is ONE underlying evidence source.

Introduce/support:

sourceOriginId
canonicalSource
derivedFrom
independentEvidenceGroup

EvidenceConfidence must consider:

source quality
independence
freshness
causal relevance

not raw article count.

15. CATALYST AI ANALYSIS
==================================================

Implement the deep Catalyst Intelligence AI analysis.

IMPORTANT:

Only run this after cheap screening indicates that the case deserves it.

Use centralized model routing.

Do NOT hardcode model names.

The strong AI should receive compact CatalystFacts.

Do not send raw complete module outputs.

Its role is qualitative reasoning.

It should answer:

1. Why could this event/setup materially affect the company?

2. Which evidence is causally relevant?

3. Are multiple independent signals aligned?

4. What is confirmed versus rumored?

5. What does the market appear to expect?

6. What could surprise the market?

7. Does price action suggest this is already priced in?

8. Is recent weakness likely temporary/cyclical/structural?

9. What is the strongest opposing interpretation?

10. What would invalidate the setup?

11. Is this interesting enough for Opportunity Finder?

27. SCHEDULED EVENT REGRESSION — SPACEX-STYLE CASE
==================================================

Create a synthetic/historical fixture representing the concept:

company has a known upcoming company meeting / technology event

before the event:

- the event itself is publicly known
- company has recently increased activity around an important strategic driver
- credible reporting/expectations suggest meaningful announcements may occur
- some positive supporting signals exist
- price has not necessarily fully run ahead

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT include secret information that was unavailable before the event.

Expected behavior:

the company should become:

EVENT_OPPORTUNITY_WATCH
or
INVESTIGATE/HIGH_INTEREST

BEFORE the event,

The test is:

"Can the system recognize that a KNOWN event deserves proactive investigation?"

not:

"Can the system magically predict the secret announcement?"
