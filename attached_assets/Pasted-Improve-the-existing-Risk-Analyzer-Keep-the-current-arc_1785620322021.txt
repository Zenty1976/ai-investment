Improve the existing Risk Analyzer. Keep the current architecture, OpenAI debug dialog, repository storage, history, manual Update Analysis behaviour and overall visual style.

Do not create another module.

The current implementation is good, but the analysis should become more portfolio-focused and less like several separate Company Monitor summaries.

--------------------------------------------------
PORTFOLIO-LEVEL RISK
--------------------------------------------------

Strengthen the OpenAI prompt with these rules:

Risk Analyzer must analyse how risks affect the portfolio as a whole.

Do not simply repeat separate company risks.

Prefer portfolio-level conclusions such as:

- simultaneous company events increasing short-term portfolio volatility
- concentration in correlated growth exposures
- sensitivity to the AI investment cycle
- interaction between macro shocks and company-specific events
- currency exposure across accounts and holdings

Company-specific risks may still be included when material, but explain their effect on total portfolio value.

Return approximately five top risks.

Normally include at least two portfolio-level risks.

Do not return more than two standalone company risks unless the portfolio genuinely justifies it.

Opportunity cost should normally be treated as a weakness or score driver, not a Top Risk, unless it is unusually material.

--------------------------------------------------
PORTFOLIO DATA PREPARATION
--------------------------------------------------

Before calling OpenAI, calculate portfolio metrics on the backend instead of asking OpenAI to infer them.

Add a compact calculated portfolio profile containing:

- total portfolio value
- total invested value
- cash percentage
- number of holdings
- position weight for every holding
- account and position currency exposure
- sector exposure when known
- largest position weight
- combined weight of the two largest positions
- number of major company events occurring within the next 14 days

Include account-level available cash and account value in the supplied context.

The current portfolio context omits available cash per account, which limits proper currency and account-risk analysis.

Do not mix account currencies when calculating totals. Use the existing base-currency values.

For each position provide:

- symbol
- company name
- market value in base currency
- portfolio weight
- currency
- sector if known
- upcoming known event if available

This should allow conclusions such as:

“Microsoft represents approximately X% of total portfolio value and Y% of invested capital.”

--------------------------------------------------
SCORE EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------

Require 5–6 scoreDrivers whenever sufficient information exists.

Include both risk-increasing and risk-reducing factors.

Examples:

Risk increasing:
- concentration
- clustered company events
- speculative holdings
- currency exposure
- correlated growth sensitivity

Risk reducing:
- cash buffer
- high-quality holdings
- exposure across more than one currency
- limited leverage

Keep the meaning:

- Positive = reduces portfolio risk
- Negative = increases portfolio risk

The risk score must describe portfolio risk, not expected investment return.
