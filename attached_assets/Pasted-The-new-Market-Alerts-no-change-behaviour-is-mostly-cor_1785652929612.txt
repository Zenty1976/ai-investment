MARKET ALERTS HISTORY

Do not use the same latest history entry for both elapsed-time calculation and alert comparison.

A NoChange history entry contains an empty alerts array, so using it as the comparison baseline causes previously known alerts to be classified as New after a no-change check.

Resolve two separate entries:

1. previousCheckEntry
- The newest history entry of any type.
- Use it for:
  - hours since previous check
  - newly detected News Monitor items
  - events passed since the previous check

2. previousMeaningfulEntry
- The newest history entry where checkResult === "Meaningful".
- Use it for:
  - previousAlerts
  - New / Updated / Unchanged classification
  - resolved-alert detection
  - previous meaningful headline and alert context

A NoChange check must never erase or replace the comparison baseline formed by the latest meaningful alerts.

Keep NoChange entries in compact history, but do not use their empty alert list as the previous meaningful alert list.
