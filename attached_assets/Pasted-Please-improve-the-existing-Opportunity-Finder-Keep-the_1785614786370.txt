PURPOSE

* Portfolio fit
* Diversification benefit
* Sector and macro fit
* Timing and catalysts
* Risk/reward
* Evidence quality

CURRENT-DATE VALIDATION

The current result included a catalyst described as “earnings expected July 28” even though the supplied date was August 1.

Strengthen the prompt with these rules:

* Never describe a past date as an upcoming catalyst.
* Compare every event date with the supplied current UTC date.
* If an event has already occurred, use the published result rather than calling it upcoming.
* Omit a catalyst when its date cannot be verified.
* Do not invent earnings dates, guidance, dividends or corporate events.
* Prefer official company investor-relations pages, exchange announcements and reputable financial sources.
* Verify ticker, company name and exchange before returning a candidate.

If a returned event date is earlier than the analysis timestamp, reject or remove it during backend validation.

* timing: integer 1–5
* riskReward: integer 1–5
* scoreReason: short explanation
* positionSizeSuitability: “Small|Medium|Large”

* confidence = evidence quality and reliability
