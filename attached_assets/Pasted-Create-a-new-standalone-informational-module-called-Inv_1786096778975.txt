Create a new standalone informational module called:

Investor Watch

This module is intentionally separate from the investment decision pipeline.

It must NOT feed data into:

* Trade Decision
* Portfolio Manager
* Risk Analyzer
* Opportunity Finder
* Trade Review

Its purpose is purely to give the user a compact overview of what a small group of notable investors currently appear to think, say or do.

The module should use OpenAI with web search to find recent, verifiable information.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Investor Watch must answer:

“What is this investor currently thinking or doing, and what has materially changed since the previous update?”

Do not create a generic biography or repeatedly summarize the person’s investment career.

Focus on:

* recent opinions
* recent interviews
* public statements
* investor letters or memos
* regulatory filings
* portfolio disclosures
* major disclosed position changes
* macro views
* sector views
* bearish/bullish themes
* material changes since the previous update

⸻

IMPORTANT SOURCE DISCIPLINE

Internet coverage of famous investors is often misleading.

The module must distinguish between:

1. Direct / Verified

Examples:

* official investor letter
* official memo
* regulatory filing such as 13F
* direct interview
* verified social-media statement
* official company/fund communication

2. Reliable secondary reporting

Examples:

* Reuters
* Bloomberg
* Financial Times
* Wall Street Journal
* CNBC when directly reporting the investor’s statement

3. Interpretation

OpenAI infers the investor’s likely view from filings, portfolio changes or several pieces of evidence.

Never present interpretation as a direct quote or confirmed opinion.

Every important item should contain:

evidenceType:
Direct | Filing | ReliableReporting | Interpretation

and:

confidence:
High | Medium | Low

Prefer primary sources wherever possible.

⸻

WHAT THEY SAY VS WHAT THEY DO

Where relevant, explicitly separate:

Says / Thinks

and

Does / Positioning

Example:

Michael Burry

Says / Thinks:
Concerned about extreme valuations in parts of the market.

Does / Positioning:
Latest available filing shows reduced exposure to X and increased exposure to Y.

Interpretation:
His positioning appears broadly consistent with a more defensive or contrarian view.

Do not assume a filing proves the investor’s exact opinion.

Remember that filings such as 13F are delayed and may not represent the current portfolio.

Always show the filing date or reporting period.

⸻

STATEFUL UPDATES

Investor Watch should behave similarly to the stateful Company Monitor.

Store the previous analysis for each person.

On later updates, ask:

* What has changed since the previous analysis?
* Is the investor’s current view materially different?
* Has a major theme strengthened or weakened?
* Is there new positioning evidence?
* Is there nothing meaningful to report?

Do not rewrite the entire analysis every time.

Possible update types:

FullAnalysis

MaterialUpdate

NoMaterialChange

NoMaterialChange is a completely valid result.

If nothing meaningful has changed:

* preserve the previous current-view summary
* update lastCheckedAt
* do not fabricate new information
* do not change themes merely to make the update look different

* Trade Decision
* Trade Review

to become deterministic evidence against a trade.

DESIGN GOAL

Investor Watch should feel like a small personal “smart money briefing”.

The main page answers:

“What are these investors thinking right now?”

The detail view answers:

“Why do we believe that, what exactly is new, and what evidence supports it?”

Keep the overview extremely compact and the detailed analysis available only when the user asks for it.
