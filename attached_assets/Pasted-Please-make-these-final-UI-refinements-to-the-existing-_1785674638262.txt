Please make these final UI refinements to the existing Trade Review module.

Do not change the current architecture, sizing logic, proposal generation, approval workflow, mock quote service, Trade Decision integration or event-blocking behaviour.

The current Trade Review now works correctly. These changes are only intended to make the page faster and clearer to read.

1. Rename the score

The circular score currently uses the label:

Strength

This is too vague.

Rename it to:

Decision strength

Keep the existing 0–100 score and calculation unchanged.

Add a short tooltip or expandable explanation:

“Decision strength measures how strongly the available analyses support this trade idea. It does not indicate whether the trade is ready to execute.”

Do not replace it with Trade Decision confidence. Confidence and decision strength are separate concepts.

2. Improve the price labels

Change:

Price

to:

Share price

Change:

Est. value

to:

Estimated trade

Example:

Share price
350.00 USD

Estimated trade
≈ 7,350 USD

Continue showing the approximate value in the portfolio base currency.

Keep the quote source and timestamp available in Details or a tooltip.

3. Make allocation labels clearer

Replace:

Target 8%
Now 0%
→ 7.9%

with clearer labels:

Current 0%
Target 8%
After trade 7.9%

Keep the same calculations.

On narrow screens, these may remain on one compact line if they fit. Otherwise wrap them cleanly without horizontal scrolling.

4. Hide module count from the main card

Remove the visible text such as:

4 modules

from the collapsed proposal card.

The number and names of supporting modules should remain available under Details.

The main card should only show information needed for a quick trade review.

5. Simplify the status presentation

The current card shows both:

* a Waiting badge
* a large event-blocked line explaining what it is waiting for

When a proposal is blocked by a named event, the event line already communicates the status clearly.

In that case:

* keep the event line
* remove the separate generic Waiting badge from the top of the card

For proposals that are waiting for another reason and have no blocking event, keep the Waiting badge.

6. Shorten the visible reason

The short reason on each collapsed card must be one concise sentence.

Maximum approximately 90 characters where practical.

Examples:

“Waiting for earnings before opening a modest position.”

“Strong earnings and improved guidance support a new position.”

“Rising risk supports reducing the current exposure.”

Do not show semicolon-separated analytical summaries in the collapsed card.

Keep the full explanation under Details.

Do not truncate by simply cutting the text mid-sentence. Generate or derive a proper short sentence.

7. Prevent mobile horizontal overflow

The current mobile view appears to have some horizontal overflow near the right side of the cards and action row.

Ensure:

* each proposal card stays within the content width
* the Decision strength circle remains fully visible
* Details does not extend outside the viewport
* action buttons wrap or resize cleanly
* no horizontal page scrollbar is introduced

The left navigation may remain fixed as it is.

8. Keep the important information compact

The collapsed proposal card should prioritize this order:

* BUY or SELL
* company and ticker
* event-blocking message when applicable
* share price
* quantity controls
* estimated trade
* current / target / after-trade allocation
* one-sentence reason
* confidence and urgency
* Approve / Later / Reject / Details
* Decision strength indicator

Do not add more explanatory text to the collapsed card.

Preserve all existing functionality:

* quantity editing
* immediate recalculation
* mock and Saxo quote support
* event blocking
* approval statuses
* Details navigation
* proposal persistence
* no automatic order placement
* phase-1 warning banner
