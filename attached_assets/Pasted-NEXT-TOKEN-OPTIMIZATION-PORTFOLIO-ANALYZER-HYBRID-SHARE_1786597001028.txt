NEXT TOKEN OPTIMIZATION — PORTFOLIO ANALYZER HYBRID + SHARED PORTFOLIO INTELLIGENCE

IMPORTANT:
Complete the Risk Analyzer hybrid task first.

Then perform this task.

Do NOT redesign the overall investment architecture.
Do NOT merge Risk Analyzer and Portfolio Analyzer into one module.

They answer different questions:

Portfolio Analyzer:
"How is my portfolio doing, what is driving it, and what deserves attention?"

Risk Analyzer:
"What risks exist in my portfolio?"

They should remain separate modules and separate UI concepts.

However, they should share deterministic portfolio calculations instead of
independently asking OpenAI to process the same raw information.

The objective is:

RAW PORTFOLIO / SAXO / SYSTEM DATA
→ SHARED DETERMINISTIC PORTFOLIO INTELLIGENCE
→ compact structured facts
→ Risk Analyzer interpretation
→ Portfolio Analyzer interpretation

OpenAI should reason about facts.

OpenAI should NOT be used as a calculator or data-processing engine.

==================================================
5. CONTRIBUTORS / DETRACTORS
==================================================

Where sufficient data exists, calculate which holdings contributed most
positively and negatively.

Prefer contribution based on portfolio weight × relevant return rather
than simply sorting stocks by percentage return.

Example:

A 2% position gaining 20% may matter less to the portfolio than a 20%
position losing 4%.

Calculate this in backend.

OpenAI should receive the result.

==================================================

SECTOR:

- current holdings

EVENTS:

- current holdings
- portfolio sectors
- major macro exposure.

==================================================
10. WHAT OPENAI SHOULD DO
==================================================

OpenAI should remain responsible for qualitative portfolio interpretation.

Examples:

- What is actually driving portfolio performance?
- Is recent performance healthy or fragile?
- Are gains broad or dependent on one/few holdings?
- Are weakening company fundamentals becoming important?
- Is the portfolio positioned reasonably for current conditions?
- Which holdings deserve attention?
- Are there important interactions between market conditions and holdings?
- What should the investor monitor next?

These are valuable AI tasks.

Do not replace them with simple thresholds.
