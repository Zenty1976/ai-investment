NEW FEATURE — CATALYST / PRE-EARNINGS INTELLIGENCE
PART 1: ARCHITECTURE, DATA FOUNDATION AND SCREENING ENGINE

BACKGROUND

The application is an AI Investor / AI Investor Command Center.

The existing architecture must be preserved:

Monitors observe the world/data
Analyzers interpret it
Opportunity Finder identifies opportunities
Trade Decision decides what should be done
Trade Review only shows trades that are actionable NOW
Automation Orchestrator controls scheduling/dependencies
Investor Watch is informational only

The system is already hybrid:
objective facts/calculations should be computed deterministically in backend code,
while OpenAI should be used for qualitative interpretation and reasoning.

DO NOT redesign or replace the existing architecture.

DO NOT create a parallel investment pipeline.

The purpose of this feature is to make the existing system capable of proactively
identifying situations such as:

"A company reports earnings in 7 days. Multiple real-world leading indicators
suggest that business conditions have improved materially faster than market
expectations, while the stock has not yet fully reflected that improvement."

This should eventually allow the system to detect high-interest PRE-EARNINGS
opportunities before the earnings release.


==================================================
1. IMPORTANT EXAMPLE / TARGET BEHAVIOR
==================================================

A.P. Møller-Mærsk is the motivating example.

Before a hypothetical earnings release, the system might observe:

- previous quarter had weak Ocean profitability
- freight rates subsequently improved
- container volumes increased
- tariff uncertainty caused companies to frontload imports
- port/capacity constraints increased
- geopolitical shipping disruption affected effective capacity
- Maersk raised guidance
- subsequent industry indicators remained strong
- earnings were approaching
- the share price had NOT already risen enough to completely remove the
  potential asymmetry

The desired system behavior would NOT be:

"Earnings soon → automatically block buying."

Instead it should recognize:

"Upcoming earnings is itself a potential catalyst."

The system should eventually distinguish:

NORMAL EVENT RISK
from
INTENTIONAL PRE-EVENT OPPORTUNITY.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT hardcode MAERSK or shipping logic into production code.

Maersk is only a regression/example case.

The architecture must work generically for Danish and US companies.


==================================================
2. CORE QUESTION
==================================================

The new intelligence should eventually answer:

"Is there credible evidence BEFORE the upcoming catalyst that company
fundamentals/business conditions are developing differently from what the
market appears to expect, and has the share price already priced this in?"

This contains several separate questions:

A. What does the market currently expect?

B. What do observable company/industry/real-world indicators suggest?

C. Is there an EXPECTATION GAP?

D. Has the stock already moved significantly in anticipation?

E. Historically, how does this company behave around earnings?

F. Is the evidence strong enough and independent enough to matter?

G. Does the resulting risk/reward justify consideration BEFORE the event?

==================================================

+

+
little pre-event run-up

14D = +22%

automatically bullish.

==================================================
13. EXPECTATION GAP
==================================================

Catalyst Intelligence should compare:

OBSERVED REALITY SIGNALS

versus

MARKET EXPECTATIONS.

Example conceptually:

Consensus has barely changed

BUT

multiple current leading indicators have improved materially

→ possible POSITIVE EXPECTATION GAP.

Alternatively:

Industry data looks excellent

BUT

analysts have already raised estimates sharply
AND
the stock is +30% in 14 days

→ much smaller opportunity.

This distinction is central to the feature.

==================================================
19. OPENAI ROLE
==================================================

Strong OpenAI should NOT calculate objective facts.

It should answer qualitative questions such as:

1. What do the combined signals imply for the upcoming catalyst?

2. Are the signals causally relevant to this company's earnings?

3. Are there multiple independent supporting signals?

4. Is evidence contradictory?

5. Does the evidence suggest a positive/negative expectation gap?

6. Does recent price action appear to have already reflected the thesis?

7. Does recent weakness appear temporary/cyclical or structural?

8. What is the strongest counterargument?

9. What evidence would invalidate the pre-event thesis?

10. Is this interesting enough to send to Opportunity Finder?
